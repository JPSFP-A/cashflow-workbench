Recommended Workflow

  1. Sign in with a Supabase user.
  2. Select or create the month.
  3. Upload the AP invoice/payment report and the two cashbook text exports.
  4. Click Process And Save Month. The raw source text and processed records are saved to Supabase.
  5. Review Outflow Validation and then clear exceptions in Map Exceptions.
  6. Save rule changes. The app writes them to Supabase and logs the change.
  7. Use Reapply Saved Rules to rebuild the month from the saved uploads after rule changes.

Procedure Logic Built In

Default rules follow your procedure: capital and inventory by FERC/FPC ranges, insurance 16501, Jameco, Office of Utilities, loan principal/interest/fees by FERC prefix, fuel by FERC and pay group, donation/sponsorship/foundation as Other, cheque categories, and cashbook CI logic. You can still override or extend these rules in the app.
