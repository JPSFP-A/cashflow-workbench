How To Use It

  1. Upload the AP invoice/payment report first. This is the main source for outflow categorization.
  2. Upload the cashbook 180 and 132 files. The app merges the clearer descriptions from 180 with the entry/account detail from 132.
  3. Click Process In Browser. The Base Case and Outflow Validation tabs update immediately.
  4. Use Map Exceptions to create rules from unmapped lines.
  5. Export the updated mapping rules CSV if you want to reuse them next month.

Procedure Logic Built In

The default rules now follow your procedure: capital and inventory by FERC/FPC ranges, insurance 16501, Jameco, Office of Utilities, loan principal/interest/fees by FERC prefix, fuel by FERC and paygroup, donation/sponsorship/foundation as Other, cashbook cost item mappings, JE27/JE09MA/JE09TA/JE26B transfer logic, and cheque category mappings.
