Recommended Workflow

  1. Sign in with your Supabase user account.
  2. Select a month in the Month field, or pick a saved month from the dropdown and click Load Month.
  3. Upload the AP invoice/payment report and the two cashbook text exports (180 and 132 formats).
  4. Click Process & Save Month. Raw source text and processed records are saved to Supabase. If the month already exists you will be prompted before overwriting.
  5. The Outflow Validation tab is shown first. Review AP outflows by base case row and by category.
  6. Switch to Map Exceptions to see unmapped or unrouted lines. Click Map on any row to pre-fill the rule form. Save the rule, then click Reapply Now.
  7. Use Reapply Saved Rules from the month panel at any time to rebuild the month from its saved uploads with the current rule set.
  8. Export the Base Case as an Excel-compatible report when the month is finalised.

Procedure Logic Built In

Default rules implement the procedure: purchase power (IPP), capital by FERC/FPC 18601 and 30000-39999, inventory 15401-15499, insurance 16501, Jameco vehicle lease, Office of Utilities regulatory fees, loan principal/interest/fees by FERC prefix 22/23/42, fuel by FERC and pay group, payroll by pay group, donations/sponsorships/foundations as Other, cashbook CI ranges (payroll 1-99, F/X 321-322, interest 352, bank charges 380/395, interest income 912), and JE27/JE09MA transfer logic.

Notes

  • Cashbook debits are the receipt side (Collections row). Cashbook credits are the bank control side only and are excluded from the Base Case.
  • AP invoice/payment report is the primary source for categorising outflows.
  • Reset / Wipe removes all records and uploads for the month and logs the action. You will need to re-upload files to reprocess after a reset.
  • To prevent public sign-ups, disable them in your Supabase project: Authentication → Settings → Disable sign-ups.
